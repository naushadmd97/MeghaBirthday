Our Story ❤️

Some moments become memories. Some memories become treasures.

The Beginning 🌸

Sometimes, the most beautiful stories begin without us even realizing it. Our story began with something so simple — our first company training call. At that time, I had no idea that the person on that call would eventually become someone so incredibly special to me. Then came the Scottish Water project, where we finally met in person. And somehow, that meeting became the beginning of something much more beautiful than just a workplace connection. From that moment, little by little, conversations turned into memories, moments turned into feelings, and a simple beginning became our journey. ❤️ Looking back now, I realize that the Scottish Water project wasn't just another project for me... It was where our story truly began. 🌸❤️ And if I could go back to that very first training call, knowing everything I know today... I would still choose to meet you. Every single time. ❤️

Our First Memory 💕

Our first Sunday alone at the office is a memory I’ll never forget. You trusted me enough to come in just for me, and that little gesture meant more to me than you probably realized. ❤️ I still remember reaching the cafeteria before you and making sure your coffee and my tea were ready, so when you arrived, we could simply sit together, have our drinks, gossip a little, and enjoy our own little world inside the office cafeteria. ☕❤️ But there’s another memory that will always have a special place in my heart — picking you up from Howrah Station at midnight. I had worked my entire shift, but I didn't care about sleep. I was too excited because, for the first time, I was going to pick up my world from a station. I had asked you so many times, “Please let me come and pick you up,” and when you finally said, “Okay, you can come,” I couldn't stop smiling. And then, finally, I saw you. ❤️ You were happy to see me, and you even gave me Thekua because you knew I was hungry. We had a little breakfast, sat together on the bus, talked, laughed, and I dropped you home. It may have looked like a simple night to everyone else... but for me, it was the night I realized how beautiful it felt to have you beside me. ❤️

That Special Moment 🥰

After waiting of **2.5 years**, meeting you in March felt like finally getting back a piece of my heart. ❤️ That first hug, my first kiss on your cheek and forehead, and simply holding your hand while we walked through **Axis Mall and Eco Park**... those moments felt unreal. After waiting for so long, I just wanted time to stop. We had lunch together, laughed, talked, wandered around, and spent hours together in our own little world. But what touched me the most wasn't where we went or what we did... It was the way you **trusted me, cared for me, and did so many little things that you had never done for anyone else — but chose to do for me.** ❤️ And then came my favorite moment... **Resting my head on your lap while you cared for me so gently.** In that moment, I didn't need anything else. I just felt safe, loved, and cared for — like I was exactly where I belonged. That day gave me my first hug, my first kiss, your hand in mine, and a memory I will carry forever. **After 2.5 years of waiting, I finally understood just how special “us” really is.** ❤️

Today ❤️

And here we are... after everything we've been through, still standing together and creating another beautiful chapter of our story. ❤️ Today, we’re not just a couple anymore. We’re like best friends, partners, and sometimes even like husband and wife — sharing our happiness, our little moments, our arguments, and our crazy days together. Yes, we fight. Sometimes we stop talking for a day, sometimes even for a week. But somehow, when we find our way back to each other, our love feels even stronger than before. And today, I know one thing for sure... You are my world. ❤️ I know you still have your insecurities and trust issues, and I know you may not feel completely comfortable with me yet. But please believe me, Megha Jaan — I’m willing to keep putting in the effort until you feel completely safe, secure, loved, and comfortable with me. I don't expect everything to become perfect overnight. I just want us to keep choosing each other, understanding each other, and growing together. And honestly, a lot of the credit for where we are today goes to you — for your patience, your understanding, and for always finding your way back to me after every fight. ❤️ Taking care of you isn't just something I want to do today. It's my responsibility, my promise, and something I want to do for you today, tomorrow, and forever. So just stay with me, stay loyal to us, and always feel comfortable being yourself with me. I promise I'll keep protecting what we have, keep choosing you, and do everything I can to never break your heart. I love you, Megha Jaan. More than these words can ever explain. ❤️♾️
